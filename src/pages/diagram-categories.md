## about the workshop

Equivariant homotopy theory studies topological spaces with the action of a group $G$, keeping track of the symmetries of that action. Much of the recent progress in the subject is organized by combinatorial and categorical data: orbit categories, Burnside categories, subgroup lattices, transfer systems, indexing systems, and related diagram categories.

This workshop focuses on that interface, highlighting the interplay among homotopy theory, group theory, category theory, and combinatorics. It also serves as a gathering place for the growing community working in [homotopical combinatorics](/writing/homotopical-combinatorics/), a field with a strong record of research with undergraduate and early graduate student coauthors.

Topics will include:

- transfer systems and $N_\infty$ operads
- model structures on posets
- Mackey and Tambara functors
- Burnside categories
- $RO(G)$-graded equivariant cohomology
- parametrized homotopy theory and algebraic patterns in higher category theory
- equivariant functor calculus
