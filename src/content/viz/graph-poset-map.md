A zoomable map of the poset of unlabeled graphs on n ≤ 7 vertices, ordered by "is isomorphic to a subgraph of" — the quotient B₍ₙ₂₎/Sₙ of Stanley's Chapter 5. Three layouts (complement-symmetric, by degree spread, by |Aut|), cover edges weighted by the multiplicities that Stanley's operator U uses on orbit sums, nodes colored by orbit size, and a panel with automorphisms, degree sequence, covers, and the complement.
