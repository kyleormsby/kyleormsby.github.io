A zoomable map of the topological spaces on n ≤ 6 points up to homeomorphism (1, 3, 9, 33, 139, 718 of them), ordered from coarser to finer: the quotient by Sₙ of the poset of topologies on [n], a subposet of B₂ⁿ, in the spirit of Stanley's Chapter 5 — except that this order is not graded, so rows (number of open sets) are only a statistic and covers jump rows. Each space is drawn either as its open sets marked on the Boolean algebra Bₙ or as the Hasse diagram of its specialization preorder; the panel shows both, with T₀-ness, connectivity, self-homeomorphisms, the opposite space (closed sets as open sets, which gives the mirror symmetry of the layout), the homotopy type (size of the Stong core) and weak homotopy type (from the order complex: a wedge of circles, S², or a disjoint union of these), and covers with orbit-sum multiplicities.
