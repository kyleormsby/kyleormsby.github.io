A zoomable map of the poset of necklaces (or bracelets) with 2, 3 or 4 colors on n beads, ordered bead by bead: the quotient of the product of chains [k−1]ⁿ by the cyclic or dihedral group, generalizing the quotients Bₙ/G of Stanley's Chapter 5 (k = 2 is exactly Bₙ/Cₙ). Complement-symmetric, lexicographic, and by-symmetry layouts; cover edges weighted by the multiplicities Stanley's operator U uses on orbit sums; nodes colored by orbit size; a panel with the stabilizer, orbit size, color runs, covers, and the complement.
