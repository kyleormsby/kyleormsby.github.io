Simple random walks on the hypercube Qₙ, one flipped coordinate at a time, animated on the cube while a tally counts how often ℓ steps bring the walker home. The exact probability — the spectral count of closed walks, 2⁻ⁿ n⁻ˡ Σ C(n,i)(n−2i)ˡ — is shown term by term beside the running frequency, so the convergence, and the odd-ℓ parity obstruction, are both visible.
