Conway's square dance: two ropes, four dancers, and the moves twist (x ↦ x + 1) and rotate (x ↦ −1/x), drawn as reduced tangle diagrams beside a zoomable Farey tessellation, where untangling is Euclid's algorithm walking the path of convergents back to ∞.
