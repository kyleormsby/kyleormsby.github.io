Bott's sphere of at-most-three-point subsets of the circle, drawn through Mostovoy's lattices: the singletons form a trefoil, the pairs a Möbius band spanning it, and a three-stage homotopy contracts the trefoil through a dunce hat of triples — the null-homotopy of S¹ → Ran₃(S¹) that Ran₂(S¹) cannot provide.
