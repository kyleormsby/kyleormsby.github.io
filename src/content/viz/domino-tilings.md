Domino tilings of the m × n checkerboard, counted exactly and flipped by hand: the 2 × n strips give the Fibonacci numbers, and Kasteleyn's formula turns every count into a product of squared distances in a grid of cosines — which specialises to a trigonometric product for the Fibonacci numbers themselves.
