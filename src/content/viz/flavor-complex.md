Ingredients from 1.8 million deduplicated recipes as a simplicial complex: a set of flavors spans a simplex when its ingredients appear together more often than chance predicts. Orbit it in 3D, slide the lift threshold to watch the Betti numbers change, trace its loops and hollow 2-spheres, and find the triples that keep it from being flag.
