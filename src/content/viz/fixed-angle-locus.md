The points that see a fixed segment AB at angle α form two circular arcs — Thales' circle when α = π/2 — and stacking those pictures over α gives the surface x² + y² − 2|y| cot α = 1, the graph of |arg((z − a)/(z − b))|, sliced in 3D with the bipolar coordinate grid drawn on it.
