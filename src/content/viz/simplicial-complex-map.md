A zoomable map of the poset of unlabeled simplicial complexes on n ≤ 5 vertices, ordered by "is isomorphic to a subcomplex of": the quotient J(Bₙ)/Sₙ of the lattice of down-sets of the Boolean algebra, in the spirit of the quotients Bₙ/G of Stanley's Chapter 5. Rank is the number of faces, the order-reversing symmetry is Alexander duality, cover edges are weighted by the multiplicities Stanley's operator U uses on orbit sums, and the panel gives the f-vector, facets, automorphisms, Alexander dual, and covers. A toggle restricts to complexes that use every vertex (the up-set above n isolated points, no longer self-dual). The width of each quotient is computed, so you can see that both versions are Sperner for n ≤ 5.
