I recently returned from a fun, scary, exhausting, and overall excellent trip to the Palisades. Good times with Josh, Patrick, and Jon, and a new longest route for myself, as Josh and I climbed the short version of [Moon Goddess Arete](http://www.supertopo.com/rock-climbing/High-Sierra-Temple-Crag-Moon-Goddess-Arete). (We rapped into the escape gully one pitch above the notch and did the "fourth class" pitches in the gully to the top.)

I'll leave a few pictures here and send you to [Josh's blog](http://sendorsand.wordpress.com/2014/05/16/temple-crag/) for a more complete trip report.
