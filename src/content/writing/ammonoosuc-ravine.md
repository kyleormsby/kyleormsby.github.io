I got to hike the Ammonoosuc Ravine Trail with friends the other weekend. (We made a loop by returning via Jewell Trail.) After enjoying soup at the Lakes of the Clouds hut (what luxury!), we were pleasantly treated to some real Mount Washington weather: sustained 50mph winds and about 15 feet of visibility.

**Internal dialogue shortly after leaving Lake of the Clouds:** How silly to have such large cairns so close together!

**Internal dialogue 30 minutes later:** Thank god these cairns are so large and close together!!! [*Grabs boulder to prevent losing balance in 70mph gust.*]
