What is drawn. A point of Ran₃(S¹) is a nonempty subset of the circle with at most three elements. Following Mostovoy, such a subset is the set of directions (doubled angles) from the vertices of an acute triangle to its circumcentre; the triangle generates a lattice in ℂ, and Weierstrass’ (g₂, g₃) of that lattice, normalised, is a point of S³ ⊂ ℂ². Singletons ↔ degenerate lattices ↔ the (2,3) torus knot g₂³ = 27g₃²; pairs ↔ rectangular lattices ↔ a Möbius band whose core is the circle of antipodal pairs (square lattices). The picture is a stereographic projection of that S³ from a point on the circle of equilateral triples (hexagonal lattices).

The homotopy γs, s ∈ [0,3], starts at the diagonal γ₀(θ) = {θ}. Stage 1 (s ≤ 1): {θ, θ + sπ} — a second point grows out of the first; the loop slides across the Möbius band and lands on its core, traversed twice. In Ran₂ this is as far as one can go: the boundary of a Möbius band is twice its core, so γ₀ is not null-homotopic there. Stage 2 (1 ≤ s ≤ 2): still inside the band, the doubled core is slid to the doubled loop m(t) = {p, p + t}, which keeps one point fixed at p = −π/2. Stage 3 (2 ≤ s ≤ 3): using a genuine third point, m contracts: {p, p + 2πr, p + 2πr + (1−r)t} with r = s − 2 — one point sits at p, a second runs once around the circle, and the third sweeps the arc between them. The image of this last stage, the set of subsets containing p, is a dunce hat {p} ∪ Ran₂(S¹) inside Ran₃(S¹).
