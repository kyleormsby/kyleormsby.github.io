Belt trick in the sphere braid group

Δ⁴ = 1 in Bn(S²)

Spinning S² by 2π drags any configuration of n points around a braid: the full twist Δ². Spinning by 4π gives Δ⁴, and that one comes undone — because the loop lifts to a closed loop in the simply connected S³.

The lift in S³

Rotations at each level lift to unit quaternions starting at 1. The 4π loop closes up into a great circle, and the untangling is that circle shrinking to the basepoint. The 2π path only reaches −1, so there is no loop to contract.
