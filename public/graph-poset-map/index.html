Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Hover a graph to see its covers; click to pin it. Edge thickness is the cover multiplicity c: the number of edges of the upper graph whose deletion gives the lower one — the coefficient in Stanley's operator U on orbit sums.

Made for Math 372: Combinatorics at Reed College, fall 2026. Elements are orbits of Sn on the edge subsets of Kn, i.e. unlabeled graphs; the order is induced from the Boolean algebra, as in Chapter 5 of Stanley's Algebraic Combinatorics. Part of e-infinity.space/viz.
