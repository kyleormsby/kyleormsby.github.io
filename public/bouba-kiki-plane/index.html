—

Drag the shape (or tap anywhere; arrow keys work too). Left↔right is shape symbolism — Köhler’s maluma/takete (1929), Ramachandran–Hubbard’s bouba/kiki (2001): rounded lobes ↔ sharp spikes. Down↔up is size symbolism — Sapir’s mil/mal (1929), where most listeners took mal for the bigger table. The nonce word above is coined on the spot: soft consonants and rounded vowels to the left, stops and front vowels to the right, i for small and a for large.
