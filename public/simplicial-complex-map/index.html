Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Hover a complex to see its covers; click to pin it. A complex is a down-set of Bn (its faces, including ∅); rank is the number of faces, so rank 0 is the void complex and rank 2n the full simplex. Edge thickness is the cover multiplicity c: the number of facets of the upper complex whose removal gives the lower one — the coefficient in Stanley's operator U on orbit sums.

Made for Math 372: Combinatorics at Reed College, fall 2026. Elements are orbits of Sn on the simplicial complexes with vertex set [n] — the down-sets of the Boolean algebra Bn, including the void complex — ordered by inclusion; the order-reversing symmetry is Alexander duality. The construction is the quotient of Chapter 5 of Stanley's Algebraic Combinatorics applied to the sub-lattice J(Bn) of B2n. Part of e-infinity.space/viz.
