Not flag: 46 empty triangles

Each pair is good, but the three together appear less often than chance (certified at 1% FDR). Their pairs are weak: in 45 of 46 the weakest pair has lift below 1.5. So they sit below the slider's range and are drawn dashed. At each t the slider's own non-flag triangles are the held-back ones, drawn solid.

    How this is built

    Lift. For a pair, lift = (nab+5)/(Eab+5), where Eab = nanb/N is the co-occurrence count expected if they were independent. The +5 shrinks rare pairs toward 1.

    Filtration. Kt contains every edge with lift ≥ t. A triangle enters with its last edge, unless the trio's own count (95% upper bound) shows its lift is below t. This holds back 730 of the 203,188 triangles at t = 1.5. Higher simplices are flag-filled. With this optimistic reading, persistence matches the flag complex. The conservative reading has 1,571 H₁ bars against 1,199, most of them born at low lift.

    Empty triangles are strict witnesses: all three pairs are significantly favored, and the trio is significantly below independence. 44 of the 46 stay significant after stratifying by website, course and dish. None stays significant inside a single dish fiber, so most non-flagness comes from mixing recipe contexts.
