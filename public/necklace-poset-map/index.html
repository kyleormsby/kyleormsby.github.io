Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Hover a necklace to see its covers; click to pin it. A necklace lies below another if some rotation of it is bead-by-bead ≤ (colors 0 < 1 < … < k−1). Edge thickness is the cover multiplicity c: the number of beads of the upper necklace whose color can be lowered by one to give the lower one — the coefficient in Stanley's operator U on orbit sums.

Made for Math 372: Combinatorics at Reed College, fall 2026. Elements are orbits of the cyclic group Cn (or the dihedral group Dn) acting on words in {0,…,k−1}n, i.e. necklaces (or bracelets); the order is induced from the product of chains, generalizing the quotients Bn/G of Chapter 5 of Stanley's Algebraic Combinatorics (k = 2 is exactly Bn/Cn).
